# Product doctrine guidance

## Purpose

Preserve the reasoning behind Jig and FLOW's purpose, product promises,
boundaries, and decision standards for a future leader.

## Ownership

- `purpose.md` owns the shared aspiration, audience hypothesis, opportunity,
  and intended experience.
- `flow.md` owns capability compounding, executable know-how, and the reasons
  for an independent method ecosystem.
- `jig.md` owns agency, power under control, and the supporting authority and
  ownership principles.
- `design-judgment.md` owns cross-product responsibilities, accepted tradeoffs,
  product proof, lessons, and open design questions.
- `../product-compass.md` is the concise shared summary and reading map.
  Specifications, engineering memory, roadmap, and field notes retain their
  existing responsibilities.

## Local Contracts

- Distinguish chosen values, testable product hypotheses, and revisable design
  commitments. Aspirations do not prove outcomes or mandate a mechanism.
- Give each detailed argument one owner. The compass summarizes and links;
  chapters cross-reference rather than repeat one another's reasoning.
- Doctrine explains accepted direction; it cannot silently authorize new
  features, public contracts, or roadmap commitments. Resolve contradictions
  with the compass explicitly in the same change.
- Changes to chosen values require owner direction. Revise product hypotheses
  and design commitments with explicit reasoning and relevant evidence;
  refine proof and judgment standards when experience improves them.
- Preserve reasons and limits, not conversational chronology or obsolete
  architecture. Keep status, coverage audits, and drafting notes in `.tmp/`.

## Work Guidance

- Write explanatory prose with a short introduction under each major heading.
  Use examples only when they clarify a decision or consequence.
- Preserve the positive product promise alongside its safeguards. Agency
  includes human and software consumers; ownership supports power under
  control. Capability compounding requires useful improvement, not just reuse.
- Extract existing reasoning before adding text. Add depth only where a
  returning leader would otherwise lose an important connection or tradeoff.
- Keep editorial instructions here, not inside the product essays.

## Verification

- Check every relative document link when moving reasoning between files, as
  required by the parent guidance.
