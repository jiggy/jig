# Expand human possibility

Jig and FLOW exist to help more people turn intentions into useful outcomes.
This shared aspiration sits above both products: **FLOW makes capability
compound; Jig enables agency through power under control.**

FLOW is an independent standard for reusable methods, called Flows. Jig is a
host that puts those methods to work under the recipient's direction. The
lasting benefit they pursue is an expansion of what people can accomplish
and the choices they can meaningfully direct. This combination is a product
hypothesis: useful applications and independent use must demonstrate its value.

## Guiding principles

Three principles connect the aspiration to what the products should enable.

1. **Let useful capability accumulate.** Make existing methods a foundation
   for accomplishing work that would otherwise be difficult or impractical.
2. **Keep capability under its recipient's direction.** Broader access should
   increase people's practical ability to choose and act.
3. **Measure progress through achievable outcomes.** More activity or
   architecture matters only when it enables useful work.

## 1. Let useful capability accumulate

Accumulation matters because access to information does not by itself supply
the means to act. Usable methods can supply those means and become
contributions others build on.

### From knowing to being able

Knowing that a problem can be solved is different from having the means to
solve it. Information can describe a good investigation, review, or building
process while leaving its reader without the time, coordination, tools, or
experience to carry it out.

Executable know-how can narrow that gap. A practitioner can share a working
method that gathers evidence, coordinates work, checks results, and knows
when to stop or ask for help. Others can apply and improve that procedure
without having to reconstruct it from advice or a previous answer.

The useful accumulation is the ability to accomplish more by applying,
combining, and improving those methods. Implementations may change quickly;
their longevity and the number of generated artifacts do not establish that
capability has grown. The gain is the additional work that becomes achievable
and the choices that become practical.

Access to such competence could help people attempt work that would otherwise
require substantially more coordination or institutional support. It does not
make professional judgment, resources, or responsibility obsolete.

### Build the builders

The project bets that a useful unit of contribution is the method that
produces and improves an outcome. A specialist might develop an excellent
evidence-review procedure without building every application that needs
evidence reviewed. Another builder could combine it with research and
synthesis methods to make an investigation achievable.

The resulting opportunity is an ecosystem of builders: people improving the
Agents, methods, tools, checks, and feedback loops that perform useful work.
Someone can concentrate on one capability while others compose it into
systems they could not readily build alone. Improvements can then contribute
to further applications, subject to evaluation in each setting. The reasoning
and limits of that compounding belong in [FLOW](flow.md).

That opportunity depends on being able to use a method without adopting its
author's entire application, provider arrangements, and execution policy.
Otherwise, combining expertise also means reconciling whole systems whose
choices may not fit the recipient's needs. Independence makes room for
specialists to contribute and for recipients to assemble their own systems.
Existing scripts, libraries, Skills, or applications remain preferable where
they already deliver the same meaningful value with less burden.

## 2. Keep capability under its recipient's direction

Greater capability expands possibility when its recipient can understand,
choose, and adapt how it is used. Practical direction requires manageable
ways to inspect work and its evidence; supervising every procedural step can
consume the very capacity delegation was meant to create.

A method author contributes expertise without acquiring the right to choose
the recipient's providers, data policy, or consequences. These responsibilities
must remain coherent when the method author, data owner, and people bearing
the consequences are different. The person capable of executing work is not
automatically entitled to choose its consequences.
Software may act under delegated authority without requiring a human at every
interface. [Jig](jig.md) develops these principles into agency through power
under control.

FLOW addresses the exchange of methods; Jig addresses putting them to work
under the recipient's direction. Their combination should make it practical
to draw on others' expertise while choosing how it acts in one's own setting.
FLOW remains independent and can serve hosts besides Jig, while Jig must
offer useful agency before a large ecosystem exists.

No mandatory registry, runtime, language, Agent, or vendor should own the
exchange of methods. Openness creates an opportunity for capability to
distribute; it does not guarantee access, affordability, quality, or adoption.
Those remain real product challenges.

## 3. Measure progress through achievable outcomes

A useful system makes valuable work achievable under meaningful direction.
Evaluate both the outcome and the burden of directing it. The intended
experience and a demanding application make that aspiration concrete enough
to examine.

### A successful ecosystem

A successful ecosystem lets someone find a useful method, understand how it
contributes, combine it with other methods, and put that system to work under
chosen powers and limits. They can inspect the outcome and its evidence,
improve a weak method, or replace it while retaining ownership of the larger
system. A person, application, or software subsystem can consume that work
within its delegated authority.

Sharing should feel as approachable as a Skill, composition as natural as a
function, and execution as governable as a process. These abilities belong
in one coherent experience: contributing a method helps someone else build,
and building with it leaves them able to understand, direct, and improve what
they have assembled.

### A demanding demonstration

The [software factory](design-judgment.md#the-software-factory-makes-the-thesis-visible)
is our north-star demonstration because it makes the combination visible:
reusable specialists work toward a reviewed patch with tests, evidence, and
bounded consequences. It gives the product thesis an outcome people can
inspect and an experience whose burden they can evaluate.

Research, incident analysis, scientific procedures, procurement review, and
personal campaigns could benefit from the same foundations. The broader
purpose applies across these fields; the factory does not define Jig's
application domain. These possibilities do not commit the products to building
every application or subsystem.

### Evidence of useful possibility

The combination of shared methods and recipient-directed execution must earn
its value in useful applications. Packaging, orchestration, Agents, and
containment contribute only insofar as they make the outcome more achievable
under meaningful direction. Technical elegance alone does not establish the
benefit. The [proof standards](design-judgment.md#what-would-count-as-proof)
define how we earn product claims.

Bad methods can spread, automation can amplify errors, and additional power
can undermine another person's agency. Useful progress must account for those
effects. Greater execution volume does not establish greater human possibility.
