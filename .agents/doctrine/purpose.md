# Purpose — expand human possibility

Jig and FLOW serve a world in which more people can turn intentions into
useful outcomes. Their shared aspiration is **Expand human possibility**.
The practical ambition beneath it is to make useful capability accumulate,
travel, and become available for further work.

This is a chosen purpose. The belief that portable agentic methods can
meaningfully advance it is a product hypothesis, to be tested through useful
applications and independent adoption.

## From knowing to being able

Knowing that a problem can be solved is different from having the means to
solve it. Information can describe a good investigation, review, or building
process while leaving its reader without the time, coordination, tools, or
experience to carry it out.

Executable know-how can narrow that gap. A practitioner can share more than an
answer or advice: a working method that gathers evidence, coordinates work,
checks results, and knows when to stop or ask for help. Others can apply that
method and improve it rather than reconstructing it from a description.

The important inheritance is accumulated capability: a better starting point
for the next person or system. Its value is the additional work they can
accomplish and the choices that become practical for them. Access to such
competence could help individuals and small teams attempt work that would
otherwise require substantially more coordination or institutional support.
It does not make professional judgment, resources, or responsibility obsolete.

## Build the builders

The project bets that a useful unit of reuse is the method that produces and
improves an outcome. A specialist might develop an excellent evidence-review
procedure without building every application that needs evidence reviewed.
Another builder could combine it with research and synthesis methods.

The resulting opportunity is an ecosystem of builders: people improving the
Agents, methods, tools, checks, and feedback loops that perform useful work.
The method becomes valuable beyond its first output or original application.
How that improvement can compound is the subject of [FLOW](flow.md).

This opportunity is lost when using a method means adopting its author's
entire application, provider arrangements, and execution policy. The problem
we address is that entanglement, not the existence of frameworks or specialized
products. An existing script, library, Skill, or application is preferable
when it already delivers the same meaningful value with less burden.

FLOW addresses the exchange of methods; Jig addresses putting them to work
under the recipient's direction. Their distinction matters: an independent
FLOW ecosystem can serve hosts besides Jig, while Jig must offer useful agency
before a large ecosystem exists.

## Who should gain from this

The initial audience hypothesis is builders whose repeated work benefits from
reusable procedures, explicit coordination, or separated responsibilities.
That includes advanced individuals as well as teams. Jig can be called by a
person, an application, or a software subsystem; a human-centered aspiration
does not require a human at every interface.

| Role | Intended experience |
| --- | --- |
| **Flow author** | Package one useful method with readable purpose and the execution, Skills, resources, schemas, or contracts it actually needs; share it without imposing a whole application stack. |
| **Application or Starter builder** | Combine specialists with sane defaults, customize meaningful choices, and introduce semantic selection only where it helps. |
| **Operator** | Choose local Agents, credentials, infrastructure, data policy, limits, and approval requirements; understand the accepted work and the host's actual guarantees. |
| **Software consumer** | Delegate bounded work, use its outcome, and handle failure under established authority without requiring interactive supervision of every step. |
| **End user** | Receive a useful outcome without confronting kernel concepts except where consent or diagnosis needs them. |

One person may occupy every human role. The design must also remain coherent
when the method author, data owner, provider, reviewer, and consequence owner
are different people or organizations. The person capable of executing work
is not automatically entitled to choose its consequences.

## A successful ecosystem

The intended experience is ordinary: find useful work, understand it, accept
an exact revision, supply local collaborators and powers, run it, and receive
evidence with the outcome. Then inspect, adapt, and reuse the method without
adopting its internal framework. Sharing should feel as approachable as a
Skill, composition as natural as a function, and execution as governable as a
process.

No mandatory registry, runtime, language, Agent, or vendor should own that
exchange. Openness creates an opportunity for capability to distribute; it
does not guarantee access, affordability, quality, or adoption. Those remain
real product challenges. The supporting ownership promise is developed in
[Jig](jig.md).

The software factory is our north-star demonstration because it makes the
whole proposition visible: reusable specialists work toward a reviewed patch
with evidence and bounded consequences. It is not the definition of Jig.
Research, incident analysis, scientific procedures, procurement review, and
personal campaigns could benefit from the same foundations. These are
possibilities, not a commitment to build every application or subsystem.

## Why the aspiration needs limits

More execution is not necessarily more useful capability. Bad methods can
spread; automation can amplify errors; additional power can undermine another
person's agency. The goal is neither maximum autonomous activity nor replacing
every human judgment. It is making useful work more achievable under meaningful
direction and responsibility.

The distinctive product bet is the combination: reusable methods that travel
and a host that lets their recipient direct their powers. Packaging,
orchestration, Agents, and containment each contribute, but technical elegance
alone does not establish the benefit of that combination. The
[proof standards](design-judgment.md#what-would-count-as-proof) determine how
we earn that claim.
