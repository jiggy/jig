# Measure CI selection before finer optimizations

Collect comparable full and proposed selective runs by change class, including
PR latency, runner wait, job minutes, candidate freeze time, host setup and audit
overhead. Enable only reviewed exclusion classes supported by fresh full audits
and the required affected checks; do not treat absence of observed misses as a
proof of complete dependencies.

After measuring remaining cost, triage integrity-verified download reuse,
same-profile preparation reuse, or finer presentation/build isolation. Preserve
ordinary workspace builds, cold installed consumers, native execution and residue
checks. Observed per-test dependencies, passing-result caches and a build-system
migration require separate evidence of complete inputs and worthwhile savings.

This records deferred investigation, not authorization or a release requirement.
