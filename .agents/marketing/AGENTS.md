# Communication materials

## Purpose

Turn capability compounding and agency into clear demonstrations for developers
building software with Agents. Own concrete promotional materials, not doctrine.

## Ownership

- `README.md` indexes video guides and future communication materials.
- Each guide owns its audience-facing story, runnable demonstration, shot order,
  and recording instructions. Source examples remain owned by `examples/`.
- `../audience.md` and `../communication.md` own strategy and editorial judgment.

## Local Contracts

- Speak to the developer and a recognizable task. Keep each presentation focused
  on one benefit with a continuous explanation and visible outcome.
- Name Skills explicitly when making that association; teaching an Agent is not
  an established synonym for installing a Skill.
- Describe actual interfaces and demonstrated results. Distinguish live execution,
  authored fixtures, injected failures, conceptual visuals, and proposed features.
- Do not turn instructions into claims of model correctness, injection immunity,
  production scale, or equivalent behavior across implementations.
- Scripts and shot lists are editable communication artifacts, not product authority.
  Keep transient run logs, recordings, and verification reports under `.tmp/`.
- Respect example complexity caps. A video does not authorize expanding its example
  into a production solution or adding capabilities merely for spectacle.

## Work Guidance

- Lead with useful work, then reveal the code/Agent method boundary through it.
- Keep operator setup accurate but distinct from the core explanation.
- Label proposed titles and spoken copy as editorial choices, not settled positioning.

## Verification

- Check relative links and execute the guide's public commands before recording.
- Rehearse live Agent paths with synthetic data, noting variation and limitations.
