# Historical engineering field notes

## Purpose

Preserves selected first-person observations and causal context that would be
lost from Git diffs or made misleading if placed in a living guide.

## Ownership

- Dated, scoped engineering retrospectives with a named recorder and evidence
  window.
- Rejected paths, observations, unresolved hypotheses, and recovery references
  whose historical context may help a future investigation.

## Local Contracts

- Every note is historical and non-normative. The product compass, current
  specifications, applicable `AGENTS.md` files, roadmap, code, and executable
  evidence always outrank it.
- Field notes are optional research material, never required maintainer re-entry
  reading and never a source of current status, blockers, interfaces, or tasks.
- Include the date, recorder or session label when known, evidence window,
  scope, and links to current canonical owners.
- Do not store credentials, personal data, legal-risk admissions, mutable
  environment facts, or claims that cannot be tied to evidence.
- Do not create a note for every agent or task. Promote one from `.tmp/` only
  when first-hand causality has durable investigative value beyond the living
  conclusions.
- Correct a materially misleading note in place or remove it. Historical
  evidence is not an excuse to preserve known falsehoods or superseded product
  contracts.

## Work Guidance

- Keep observations distinct from conclusions and unresolved hypotheses.
- Point to Git landmarks instead of copying deleted implementations.
- Distill any enduring rule into its living owner; do not make readers infer
  current policy from a retrospective.

## Verification

- Check links and Git landmarks named by a new or changed note.
