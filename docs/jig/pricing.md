<div className="pricing-faq">
<h3>Common questions</h3>
<details>
<summary>What counts as annual revenue?</summary>
<p>Revenue means your consolidated group's revenue for its last completed financial year using standard accounting rules. It does not include investment capital, funding rounds, company valuation, or employee headcount.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What counts as a "group"?</summary>
<p>Your ultimate controlling parent and all entities it controls (more than 50% voting rights). A sole trader or independent business is its own group. Passive minority investments do not merge groups.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What happens if our company revenue grows past $1M?</summary>
<p>You assess eligibility once a year. If you cross $1M, you have a 90-day transition period to acquire a company license or discontinue operational use. Earlier free use remains completely valid and free.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Can our supplier arrange our purchase for us?</summary>
<p>Yes. A supplier or consultancy can arrange the purchase naming your group as the customer. The authorized seller (Pereirinhos s.r.o.) sends the coverage confirmation directly to you.</p>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What about FLOW, Sley, and independent Flows?</summary>
<p>FLOW, Sley, third-party libraries, and Flows you write retain their own licenses. Bread covers only the Jig host software specified in the license adoption notice.</p>
</details>
