# Research

These pages record hypotheses, proposed methods, and the evidence needed to evaluate them.
They describe possibilities rather than current commands, supported capabilities, or roadmap commitments.

- [Use cases](./use-cases.md) organize possible applications by their intended outcome.
- [Orchestration patterns](./orchestration-patterns.md) examine structures that might justify reusable methods.
- [Time-travel handoff](./time-travel-handoff.md) examines one context-transfer hypothesis.

For implemented behavior, use [Build](../build/index.md) and the [complete examples](../examples/index.md).
Exact requirements belong in [Reference](../reference/index.md).
