# Jig pricing under Bread

**One company purchase. Permanent rights to covered releases. Twelve months of new official releases.**

**Jig schedule P-1.0 — effective 2026-09-21.** These are Jig's prices, not
universal Bread prices. [Jig's adoption notice](LICENSE.md#jig-adoption-notice)
identifies the Licensor, authorized seller, and covered source. Email
**licensing@jig.md** for an ordinary purchase or a supplier-arranged customer order.

## Price

| Consolidated Group annual revenue, USD | Twelve-month purchase |
| --- | ---: |
| Below $1 million | Free business use |
| $1 million–under $10 million | $100 |
| $10 million–under $100 million | $1,000 |
| $100 million or more | $10,000 |

Revenue means the last completed financial year's consolidated revenue, using
[the license's assessment rules](LICENSE.md#3-business-eligibility), not funding,
valuation, headcount, or revenue attributable to Jig. Declare a band; no routine
accounts upload is required. At each tenfold revenue boundary from $1 million,
the price rises tenfold from $100, capped at $10,000. Taxes are additional and
shown before purchase.

Personal non-business use and genuine evaluation are free without registration.
A sole trader's business follows the same revenue threshold as other businesses.
The full software is available to everyone. There are no seats, machine counts,
usage fees, product royalties, or publisher coverage charges.

## What the purchase covers

One purchase covers the named Group across all its Jig applications and
installations. It includes existing official Jig releases and releases first
published during its twelve-month Update Term, subject to specific existing
exclusions identified before purchase. Dates are inclusive at the start and
exclusive at the end, in UTC.

Those release rights last for the duration of copyright, including permitted
independent modifications. Nonrenewal and later revenue growth do not reprice
or cancel them. Another application using a covered release requires no new
Jig purchase; a newer uncovered official release does, including a security
update. No future release or support service is promised.

The purchase is twelve months, normally paid upfront. There is no one-month
purchase. An expressly accepted credit order is an annual obligation, not a
collection of monthly grants. A seller's optional marketing or service bundles
do not change Bread's rights or minimum term.

## Purchase and distribution

Provide the Group and controlling parent's legal identity, billing contact,
declared band, and requested start date to the official purchasing channel in
the applicable Jig adoption notice. The seller provides the total, terms, and
coverage record; the business purchaser must have authority to accept the order.

A supplier can arrange the same purchase naming its customer. The upstream
seller receives payment or expressly accepts credit and confirms coverage
directly to that customer. Paying the supplier alone does not create a Jig
license. The supplier may advance the upstream payment or direct the customer
to pay upstream. Its own license never covers unrelated customers.

Redistribute free or paid products with Jig's license, release identification,
source and notices. Customers use their own applicable permission; distributors
need not audit customers or report sales. Separate installation of Jig does not
change this rule.

An independent hosting provider uses its own Group's permission; customers need
none merely to access the service. A supplier administering a customer's
controlled deployment uses that customer's permission. Exporting a copy for
independent operation requires the recipient's applicable permission.

Future prices follow Bread's advance-notice rule and affect only future
purchases. Free users owe no support, promotion, or contribution. FLOW, Sley,
independent applications and separately licensed dependencies keep their terms.
