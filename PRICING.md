# Jig pricing under the Bread License

**One license. Your whole company. From US$10 a year.**

**Schedule P-1.0 — effective 2026-09-21.** These are Jig's prices, not universal
Bread prices. Accepted orders identify this schedule and their Update Term.
The [license](LICENSE.md) defines the permissions; the
[Jig adoption notice](LICENSE.md#jig-adoption-notice) identifies the owner,
covered material and authorized seller.

Email **licensing@jig.md** to buy coverage or enroll a paid product under the
standard publisher agreement. For a direct order, provide the company-group
identity, billing details, financial band, and annual or monthly Update Term.
The seller supplies the total with applicable taxes and an order you can keep;
permission starts on payment or express acceptance of an invoice order under
section 4. Publisher enrollment follows section 6 and needs no sales approval.

Jig is one complete product. Personal use is free. Small companies qualify for
free use below the threshold shown below. Genuine evaluation is free for anyone:
no registration, countdown, or commitment to buy. Once you adopt Jig for routine
business work, you need the appropriate business or included product permission.
Employer policies still apply.

## Run Jig for your company

Add your company group's revenue and qualifying funding, without double counting.
The license calls this **Financial Scale**. Its [financial rules](LICENSE.md#3-organization-eligibility)
define the controlled group, assessment period, and which funding counts.

| Company-group revenue + qualifying funding, USD per year | Annual price |
| --- | ---: |
| Below $1 million | Free ordinary use |
| $1 million–under $10 million | $10 |
| $10 million–under $100 million | $100 |
| $100 million–under $1 billion | $1,000 |
| $1 billion or more | $10,000 |

Starting at $1 million, each tenfold band increases the fee tenfold, up to the
$10,000 launch maximum. Taxes are additional and shown before purchase. Declare
your band; no routine upload of accounts. No charges per person, Agent,
execution, or machine.

### Use it now. Keep using it later.

An annual purchase covers your company group's ordinary use of existing official
Jig versions and any published during the following twelve months. Any existing
material excluded from coverage must be identified before purchase. The license
calls that twelve-month period the **Update Term**.

Stopping renewal leaves the period you already paid for intact. Updates
published after that period ends are not covered by that purchase. You can keep
using software already covered.

Coverage includes subsidiaries later acquired by the named parent. If an entity
leaves the group, it keeps its purchased update period and lasting permissions
for its own operations. Those rights do not extend to its new parent's other
operations.

### Prefer monthly sponsorship?

A company sponsorship can buy the same rights one paid month at a time. Its
licensing portion is the annual fee divided by twelve, rounded up to the nearest
cent. Any additional support or benefits are shown before purchase. There is no
hidden minimum support contribution.

An individual's contribution does not license their employer. Support-only
sponsorship adds no software permission; it can accompany an existing license
without paying twice for the same coverage.

Future prices follow the license's notice and annual-review rules. They cannot
change an already-paid period or revoke permissions already acquired.

## Redistribute or host Jig with customer licenses included

Accept the published publisher agreement, identify your paid product or service,
and pay **1% of its covered receipts**. No negotiation, upfront charge, minimum
payment, or cloud premium. Your customers receive a Jig license for that product
or service, regardless of company size—not for unrelated Jig deployments.

The agreement explains which receipts count, including taxes, refunds,
pass-through compute, and bundles. **Report and pay once a year—or more often
if that suits you.** Annual reporting is the default. At enrollment, you can
choose a shorter period in whole calendar months. Periods align with the
calendar year; the last one ends on December 31. Reports and payments are due
within 30 days after each period ends. Change frequency for the next calendar year by giving
notice before it starts. No customer roster or runtime telemetry. The rate stays fixed for the enrolled product and its
normal updates. Official Jig versions remain included while enrollment continues.
If your business closes, customers keep their acquired rights to delivered copies.

### What about free products and plans?

Recipients of free Jig copies or customer Jig environments need their own
permission, through free eligibility or another applicable grant. Operating a
free plan also requires your own permission. A paid plan does not cover free
users, and no blanket-coverage purchase is offered for free products.

### What can customers do with the product?

They can run and modify it, add Flows, and repair its Jig component. They cannot
extract Jig to license a separate deployment. Supply source for distributed Jig
code and your changes; independent application code can stay private.

Existing paid-product permissions remain valid. Issuing new customer coverage
requires continuing publisher permission; passing on coverage already issued
for resale does not require a new enrollment. Repairs and updates using only a
customer's already-covered Jig version need no new coverage. Existing hosting
periods may use Jig versions covered before enrollment ended.

You can also distribute Jig with clear notice that customers supply their own
permission instead of including it in your product.

## Use a hosted application

Getting answers or results from an app does not require you to buy Jig merely
because Jig runs behind the scenes. The operator needs coverage.

A service that runs your own Flows or systems provides a customer Jig environment,
even if the provider manages execution. Merely configuring an app's existing
features does not. A provider can include Jig permission with a paid service;
a free environment follows ordinary eligibility. Renting a generic VM supplies
no Jig license. To export and run Jig yourself, you need explicitly included
permission for that copy or your own applicable Jig license.

## Your work stays yours

No Jig royalty merely for outputs or independently authored Flows. No activation
server, compulsory licensing telemetry, or remote licensing shutdown. FLOW and
Sley keep their own licenses. Jig's Bread license is source-available,
not OSI-approved open source.
